Project Purpose

Why Nexus Guard?

The internet is full of unfiltered visual content. Nexus Guard provides an accessible, AI‑driven layer of protection that can be integrated into social platforms, educational sites, parental control tools, or any web environment where safety matters.

Developed as an MCA 4th Semester Major Project, this system demonstrates practical application of deep learning, Flask, REST APIs, and browser‑extension technology.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does NSFW mean?

“Not Safe For Work” – content that is sexually explicit, violent, or otherwise inappropriate for public viewing.

Which AI model is used?

We use EfficientNet‑B0 fine‑tuned on a curated dataset of safe and unsafe images, achieving over 95% validation accuracy.

Can I use this as a browser extension?

Yes! The companion Chrome extension scans all images on a page and blurs those detected as NSFW.

Are my images kept?

No. Uploaded images are processed in memory and deleted immediately after analysis.
